# Written Action of the Founder — Management Authorization

**Company:** Abandoned Asset OS (the "Company")
**Subject:** Authorization and funding of internal-use software development
**Effective (Management Commitment) Date:** see `management_authorization_date` in `audit_log.json` (date of first commit in the repository)

## Recitals

The undersigned, acting as the sole founder and manager of the Company, hereby adopts the
following action in lieu of a meeting, effective as of the date above.

## Resolutions

1. **Authorization.** The Company authorizes the design, development, and deployment of an
   internal-use software platform consisting of an institutional asset clearinghouse,
   machine-to-machine settlement protocol, multi-tenant routing engine, payments and escrow
   integration, autonomous ingestion and underwriting services, and associated administrative
   terminals (the "Platform").

2. **Funding.** The Company commits to funding the Platform's application development stage,
   including founder labor performed in the capacity of Senior Systems Architect.

3. **Probability of Completion.** Management has determined it is probable that the Platform
   will be completed and used for its intended internal business function.

4. **Capitalization Policy.** Application development stage costs are capitalized as an
   intangible asset in accordance with US GAAP ASC 350-40 and ASU 2025-06. Preliminary project
   stage and post-implementation costs are expensed as incurred.

5. **Labor Rate Benchmark.** Founder engineering labor is valued at the market benchmark rate of
   **$150.00 per hour** for full-stack systems architecture, API clearinghouse engineering, and
   multi-tenant platform logic.

6. **Evidentiary Record.** Hours and capitalized value are recorded in
   `/docs/capitalization_ledger/development_log.md` and `/docs/capitalization_ledger/audit_log.json`.
   Every entry is anchored to an immutable Git commit SHA-1/SHA-256 hash, which provides
   cryptographic evidence of the date and content of the work performed.

7. **Ratification.** All development activity performed prior to the execution of this action is
   hereby ratified and approved as of the Effective Date.
